# grabnr logo prompts

Short descriptions to paste into an image generator or hand to a designer.

## Logo mark (short)

A bold, flat vector letter "G" in a blue gradient (light sky blue top-left to deep royal blue bottom-right). The left side of the G is sliced by horizontal speed-line gaps and trails small dots, suggesting fast, split data. The G's crossbar is a thick down arrow, meaning download. Clean geometric shapes, rounded joins, no outline, no shadow, transparent background.

## Full lockup

The mark above, centered over the lowercase wordmark "grabnr" in a heavy geometric sans (like Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold). "grab" is near-black navy (#0E1128), "nr" is bright blue (#1F7BFF). Under it, in light slate grey, the tagline "Split. Download. Go faster." White background, generous padding.

## App icon

Rounded-square app icon (macOS style, 1024 px). Off-white to pale blue background with the blue gradient G mark centered at about 70% of the icon width. Flat, no glossy effects.

## GitHub social preview (1280x640)

Dark navy (#0E1128) background. Mark on the left, wordmark "grabnr" on the right in white with "nr" in light blue, and two lines below in muted lavender: "Download faster by using every connection at once." and "Wi-Fi, Ethernet, USB and phone tethering. One file."
